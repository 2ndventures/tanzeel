Tanzeel Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

2nd Ventures, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Tanzeel mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Tanzeel.

Information We Collect

Tanzeel does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our servers.

App functionality operates on your device using local storage. This includes:

  • Reading progress and bookmarks
  • User preferences such as theme, font size, and reciter selection
  • Reading statistics and history
  • Application settings

This information is stored exclusively on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Third-Party Services

Quran.com API

The App uses Quran.com services to provide Quran content, audio recitation, and word-level timing data. When you request content or play audio, your device may connect directly to Quran.com or its content delivery network.

Those connections may expose your IP address to Quran.com's servers. Please review Quran.com's privacy information for details about its handling of data.

Data Storage and Security

User data described above remains on your device under your control. You can delete it by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Tanzeel is suitable for users of all ages. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, your access, deletion, portability, and opt-out rights are protected by keeping your App data on your device.

International Data Transfers

We do not transfer personal data internationally because we do not collect or store personal data. Connections from your device to third-party content providers are governed by those providers' privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
