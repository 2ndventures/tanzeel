PRIVACY POLICY
2nd Ventures, LLC — Tanzeel
Last Updated: November 14, 2025

================================================================================
INTRODUCTION
================================================================================
2nd Ventures, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Tanzeel mobile application
(the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection,
use, and disclosure of information when you use our App. We are committed to
protecting your privacy and complying with applicable privacy laws, including GDPR
(General Data Protection Regulation) and CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act).

================================================================================
INFORMATION WE COLLECT
================================================================================
Tanzeel does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

All app functionality operates entirely on your device using local storage.
This includes:
  - Reading progress and bookmarks
  - User preferences (theme, font size, reciter selection)
  - Reading statistics and history
  - Application settings

This data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to us or
any third party.

================================================================================
THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
================================================================================
The App uses the following third-party service:

Quran.com API
  We use the Quran.com API to provide audio recitation and word-level timing
  data. When you play audio in the app, your device connects directly to
  Quran.com's content delivery network to stream the audio files.

  This connection may expose your IP address to Quran.com's servers. We
  recommend reviewing Quran.com's privacy policy for information about how they
  handle this data.

================================================================================
DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY
================================================================================
All user data is stored locally on your device using your device's native storage
mechanisms. This data remains under your control and can be deleted at any time
by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data through your device settings.

================================================================================
IN-APP PURCHASES
================================================================================
The App may offer a one-time purchase option in the future. If implemented, all
purchase transactions will be processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play
Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. Please refer to Apple's
or Google's privacy policies for information about how they handle payment data.

================================================================================
CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
================================================================================
Our App is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because we do not
collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children
under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). All data is stored locally
on the device and remains under the control of the device owner.

================================================================================
YOUR RIGHTS
================================================================================
Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, the following
rights are inherently protected:

  - Right to Access: All your data is stored on your device and accessible to
    you at all times.
  - Right to Delete: You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app or
    clearing app data in device settings.
  - Right to Portability: Your data never leaves your device.
  - Right to Opt-Out: No data collection means no opt-out is necessary.

================================================================================
INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS
================================================================================
We do not transfer any personal data internationally because we do not collect or
store personal data. The only data transfer that occurs is when your device
connects to Quran.com's servers to stream audio content, which is governed by
Quran.com's privacy policy.

================================================================================
CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
================================================================================
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any
changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the
"Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically
for any changes. Changes are effective when posted in the app.

================================================================================
GDPR COMPLIANCE
================================================================================
For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with GDPR requirements.
Since we do not collect personal data, most GDPR obligations do not apply.
However, we maintain this privacy policy to ensure transparency about our
practices.

================================================================================
CCPA COMPLIANCE
================================================================================
For California residents, we comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act
(CCPA). We do not:
  - Collect personal information
  - Sell personal information
  - Share personal information for behavioral advertising
  - Retain personal information

Therefore, the rights to opt-out, delete, and access do not apply as there is no
data collection.
